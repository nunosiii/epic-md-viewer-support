Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

Epic MD Viewer collects no personal data, sends nothing to the internet, and contains no analytics or tracking of any kind.

What data we collect

None. Epic MD Viewer is a local file viewer. It opens Markdown files from your Mac and renders them on-screen. No information about you, your files, or your usage ever leaves your device.

Network access

Epic MD Viewer does not make any network requests. It has no server, no backend, and no cloud component. The app is fully sandboxed and operates entirely offline.

Files and documents

The app reads only the Markdown files you explicitly open. It does not scan your file system, index your documents, or transmit file contents anywhere. Files are processed entirely on-device.

Recent documents

The app stores security-scoped bookmarks to recently opened files using your Mac's local storage (UserDefaults). This list never leaves your device and is not shared with anyone.

Preferences

Your appearance preference (Light / Dark / System) is saved locally using macOS UserDefaults. This data stays on your device.

Third-party SDKs and analytics

Epic MD Viewer contains no third-party analytics, crash reporting SDKs, advertising frameworks, or tracking libraries.

Children's privacy

Epic MD Viewer does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The app is rated 4+ on the Mac App Store.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
